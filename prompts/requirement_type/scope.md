## Which norms carry a requirement type (DEC-19)

A norm carries a requirement_type only when all three hold: its
deontic_type is "obligation" or "prohibition"; its source unit is outside
Articles 5, 6 and 7 and outside the annexes; and its actor, explicit or
inferred, is an operator (provider, deployer, importer, distributor,
authorised representative, product manufacturer, operators in general, or
an actor the text leaves unspecified). Every other norm has
requirement_type null, read "not an operator requirement": a definition, a
right, a permission or an exemption; a norm in Articles 5 to 7 or in an
annex; a norm addressed to the Commission, the AI Office, a Member State,
a notifying authority, a market surveillance authority, a notified body or
an affected person.
