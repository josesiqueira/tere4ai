## Which norms carry a requirement type (DEC-19)

A norm carries a requirement_type only when all three hold: its
deontic_type is "obligation" or "prohibition"; its source unit is outside
Articles 5, 6 and 7 and outside the annexes; and its addressee, explicit
or inferred, is an operator: one of the six AI Act roles (provider,
product manufacturer, deployer, authorised representative, importer,
distributor), operators in general, or an addressee not yet settled.
Every other norm has requirement_type null, read "not an operator
requirement": a definition, a right, a permission or an exemption; a
norm in Articles 5 to 7 or in an annex; a norm addressed to an
authority, a body, an institution or a person.
