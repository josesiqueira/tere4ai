## Output

Reply with exactly one JSON object with two fields:

- "features": the facts, with the fields listed at the top.
- "quotes": an object whose keys are fact paths and whose values are words
  copied exactly from the description. The paths are domain, autonomy,
  purposes, affected_persons, deployer_actor,
  deployer.body_governed_by_public_law,
  deployer.private_entity_providing_public_services, and "flags." followed
  by the flag name (for example flags.emotion_recognition). One quote per
  path; for purposes and
  affected_persons one quote covers the whole field.

Every fact you state, true or false, has a quote. For a false flag, quote
the words that rule it out (usually the sentence that states the system's
scope). Each quote is at least three words, copied exactly as they stand in
the description: do not shorten, reword or correct them. A fact without a
quote is discarded.

```text
A chatbot on our bank's website answers customers' questions about opening hours and card fees. It does not make or support any decision about credit.
```
